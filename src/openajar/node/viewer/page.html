    Stop the agent?

    The agent finishes the step it is on and then stops. What it runs here is cancelled, and nothing runs until you take control or hand it back.

    Paste text into the screen

    The text is typed in where the cursor is on the screen, so click that place first. Pasting with the keyboard does the same without this window.

    Keyboard shortcuts

    They work even while your keys go to the screen.

    T
    Take over
    H
    Hand back to the agent
    S
    Stop agent
    A
    Show or hide agent activity
    F
    Full screen (also F while you watch)
    R
    Record a clip, and stop it (also R while you watch)
